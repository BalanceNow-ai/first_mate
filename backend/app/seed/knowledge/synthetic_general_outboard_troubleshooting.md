# Synthetic general outboard troubleshooting

## Hard starting

When an outboard engine is hard to start, this synthetic checklist suggests checking that the fuel tank vent is open, the fuel line primer bulb is firm, the kill-switch lanyard is fitted and the battery is charged. Fouled spark plugs and a blocked fuel filter are common causes of hard starting.

## Overheating

If the tell-tale stream is weak or the overheat alarm sounds, this synthetic checklist suggests stopping the engine, checking the water intake for weed or plastic bags, and inspecting the water pump impeller and thermostat.

## Loss of power

A sudden loss of power can come from a damaged propeller, water in the fuel, or a clogged fuel filter. This synthetic checklist suggests checking the propeller for damage and the fuel filter bowl for water.
