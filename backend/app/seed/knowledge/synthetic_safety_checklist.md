# Synthetic pre-departure safety checklist

## Lifejackets

This synthetic checklist item asks the skipper to count one lifejacket per person on board, check each lifejacket's buckles and straps, and confirm the lifejackets are within their service date.

## Communications and distress

This synthetic checklist item asks the skipper to check that the VHF radio powers on, that the EPIRB or PLB battery is in date, and that flares are stored dry and within their expiry date.

## Weather and trip plan

This synthetic checklist item asks the skipper to check the marine forecast and to tell someone ashore the trip plan and expected return time before departure.
